# The paid layer — what's waiting to be built

Decided, not built. Nothing here starts until the free offer is polished
and the legal structure (micro-entreprise via Pépite/SNEE) exists — no
payment can be taken before that. The governing rule is `RULES.md`, "The
free/paid line": the daily ritual is free forever; what grows over time is
paid.

Last updated: 2026-10-14.

## Selfinder+ (one subscription)
- Your whole Arc (the free tier keeps the last 14 days).
- Intentions — the future cone, arrived at through Possible Selves and
  The Threshold (`docs/your-arc-concept.md`, steps 3–4).
- All twelve Journeys, their results woven into your past (the first
  Journey free).
- Unlimited conversation (the free tier has a generous daily limit).
- A pattern from your history (Center).
- €49.99/year or €7.99/month, 7-day trial on the yearly plan.
- Build the free limits as switches that start *off*, so turning
  payments on is a setting, not a rebuild.

## Where it's sold (decided 2026-10-13)
- **The website is the main shop:** Stripe checkout on selfinder.online
  for Selfinder+, gift codes, a founding membership and the digital books.
  Sales that start outside the app carry no store fee (~3% card
  processing only).
- **The app also sells, as the stores require:** in-app purchase in every
  country except Russia (Apple's 3.1.3(b) expects it alongside web
  purchases), plus a "Buy on selfinder.online" link where the stores allow
  it — iPhone US (0% for now) and EU (~10–15% within 7 days of the tap),
  Android US/EU/UK (~10% on subscriptions). Re-check these fees before
  building — they were changing through 2026.
- **One account, one subscription:** web, Apple and Google purchases all
  write to the same backend record (`arcSubscription`, `source:
  'web'|'apple'|'google'`).
- **Russia: free only** — no offers shown (storefront check).
- **To update when built:** `RULES.md` names StoreKit and Play Billing as
  the payment paths — add Stripe as a payment processor there and in the
  privacy policy (it sees only the payment, never readings).

## One-off and digital products, later
- **Your pattern, to keep:** Center's kaleidoscope as a high-resolution
  phone wallpaper and printable file. Small one-off (€2–4, in-app — too
  small for web checkout friction) or part of Selfinder+; decide when
  building ("one product, not a menu" argues for Selfinder+).
- **The seventeen states as digital books and printables:** an e-book of
  the state pages, edited and designed; a printable journal (the four
  sphere questions, the seventeen states with their symbols, pages for
  your own words). Sold on the website, Gumroad or Etsy.
- **Gift codes** ("a month / a year of Selfinder+"), on the website.
- **A founding membership:** lifetime Selfinder+ for a one-off price,
  limited to the first few hundred, sold on the website before launch —
  honest about what exists and what's coming, no countdowns.

## Considered and parked
- Physical deck of the seventeen states, printed year-in-Arc book, ritual
  kit; online circles and retreats; coach sharing; universities and team
  licences (zero reporting only); session music on streaming — good, but
  too early.
- Guided programmes (e.g. "Seven evenings with Marcus") would be paid
  *content*, which `RULES.md` rules out as paid depth — only as a separate
  product beside the app, and only if decided explicitly.
- Never: ads, selling or showing anyone's states to a third party
  (employers, insurers, "team mood"), affiliate placement inside the app.
