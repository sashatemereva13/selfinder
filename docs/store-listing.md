# Selfinder — store listing & online copy

Outward-facing copy for the App Store, Google Play, ads and social. It
follows `docs/pitch.md`'s rule — **describe the door, not the destination**:
lead with what people already search for and already feel, then say
plainly what the app does.

**The language split (2026-10-08).** Outside the app, where people search,
Selfinder uses the phrase they're already looking for: **"raise your
vibration."** Inside the app, the product says **"shift toward love"** —
no "raise," "up," or "higher" on any screen (RULES.md, "Shift has
destinations, not a summit"). The listing is the door; the app is the room.
So: "raise your vibration" belongs in names, keywords, headlines and hooks;
the body copy describes what actually happens in the app, in the app's own
words.

---

## Before publishing — check these

- **Every ⚑-marked line describes the guided Shift session, which isn't
  built yet.** App Store guideline 2.3 (accurate metadata) rejects listings
  that promise features the app doesn't have. Publish ⚑ lines only once the
  session ships; until then use the "Today's version" description below.
- **No health or medical claims, anywhere.** No "reduces anxiety,"
  "treats," "heals," "therapy." Selfinder is practice, not therapy (RULES.md,
  Content/voice) — the App Store line and the honest line are the same line.
- **No promised outcomes.** Say what the app does ("guides you,"
  "shows you where you stand"), never what it will do to someone ("you
  will feel loved," "guaranteed").
- **The 17-state map is a map, not a measurement** (`docs/pitch.md`, "Is
  the vibration scale scientific?"). Never imply it measures a physical
  vibration.
- **"Free" is true today** (RULES.md: fully free, no gates). Rewrite these
  lines if paid features return.
- Character limits are noted per field; counts below are checked.

**App name** (max 30)

**Subtitle** (max 30)
> Philosophers guide you to love

**Promotional text** (max 170 — editable any time without a new release)
> Find out where you stand in four questions. Then let Socrates, Marcus
> Aurelius or Camus guide you toward love — six minutes, in your pocket. ⚑

*Today's version (until the session ships):*
> Four questions show where you stand on a map of 17 states. Then talk it
> through with Socrates, Marcus Aurelius, Camus, Kierkegaard or Aristotle.

**Keywords** (max 100, comma-separated, no spaces — Apple already indexes
the name and subtitle, so those words aren't repeated here)
> raise,frequency,manifest,energy,mood,stoic,meditation,journal,mindful,calm,self,growth,emotions

**Description** (max 4000)

> **Raise your vibration — with a philosopher at your side.**
>
> Most apps tell you how to feel. Selfinder starts by asking where you
> actually are.
>
> **Find where you stand.** Four short questions — about your body, mind,
> heart and spirit — place you on a map of 17 states, from shame to peace.
> Not a score, not a verdict: a position, like a dot on a map. Every state
> has a page explaining what it feels like, where you'll see it in the
> world, and what it's for.
>
> **Shift toward love.** ⚑ Press one button and your philosopher walks
> with you from where you are toward love: a few slow breaths, a moment of
> warmth you already know, then letting it widen. Six minutes, with sound
> underneath. At the end you place yourself on the map again — and see the
> distance you travelled.
>
> **Walk with a real philosopher.** Choose who guides you: Socrates, who
> asks until you hear your own answer; Marcus Aurelius, a Roman emperor's
> private notebook; Kierkegaard on choice and anxiety; Camus on living
> with what makes no sense; Aristotle, who turns everything into practice.
> Each speaks in his own voice — and each is there to ask, never to tell
> you who you are.
>
> **Say what you never say out loud.** Talk it through in conversation, or
> write freely for one minute with no one watching. The answers were
> always yours — Selfinder just makes room to hear them.
>
> **Watch your arc form.** Every reading becomes a point. Over weeks, the
> points draw the shape of your inner weather — a record of what happened,
> in your own words, never an analysis of you.
>
> **More ways in:**
> • Tune In — sound to settle into
> • Breathing — guided breath
> • Journeys — question paths into what you're really carrying: control,
>   choice, the loops you keep repeating
> • Cards — draw a symbol for right now
> • A message for right now, when you'd rather be found than look
>
> **Made to feel like an evening by the fire, not another app.**
> No streaks. No badges. No guilt for missing a day. Your words stay
> yours: first-party analytics only, no ad tracking, and you can export or
> delete everything.
>
> **Free.** In English and Russian.
>
> Selfinder is a practice for self-reflection, not therapy or medical
> care. If you're struggling, please reach out to a professional or a local
> helpline.
