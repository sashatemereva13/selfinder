# Selfinder physics rules

## Prime law

The image documents a phenomenon; it does not decorate a statement. Every visible element must answer: **which field property caused this?** If the answer is only “it looks good,” remove it.

## Invariants

These never change between vibrations:

- the observer remains genderless, still, and centered; its silhouette and
  aura share the active vibration color while its chest core retains an ivory
  point of light;
- the universe begins at `#06060d`, never pure black;
- one scene uses one level color plus ivory—no competing palette;
- geometry is hairline, restrained, legible in grayscale, and never a glowing neon blob;
- the camera observes slowly; it does not perform cinematic tricks;
- movement gathers, condenses, synchronizes, resists, or releases for a stated reason;
- labels resemble research notes, not a futuristic HUD;
- no numeric score appears as a hierarchy or progress meter;
- no scientific claim: this is an internally consistent fictional cosmology.

## Definition contract

Every state is authored through the same 16 properties and no level-specific
rendering code: gravity, spatial coherence, entropy, resonance, information
flow, permeability, time behavior, field lines, particle behavior, dominant
geometry, light behavior, camera behavior, motion profile, shader behavior,
audio behavior, and interaction behavior. The canonical typed contract lives in
`src/types.ts`; all 17 complete DNA records live in `src/fields.ts`.

Values are normalized from 0–1 except gravity (-1 inward to +1 outward). A level is not “better” because a value is larger. Each combination creates a different habitat.

No parameter is conceptually binary. Named modes select attractors, but their
numeric strength, morph, continuity, integrity, visibility, and fluidity values
remain interpolable. A future transition view must blend these values and the
source/target geometry positions over time; it must never cut from one complete
scene to another.

## Visual causality

- coherence controls how faithfully particles retain a shared form;
- gravity controls inward collapse versus outward release;
- entropy introduces structured deviation, never frame-by-frame randomness;
- connectivity controls local synchronization and visible relationships;
- permeability controls whether particles cross the observer boundary;
- resonance controls how strongly nearby lines share rhythm;
- time changes rate and continuity, not merely playback speed;
- geometry establishes the field's stable attractor;
- color is assigned last and cannot carry the concept alone.

## Recursive substrate

The world is a ray-marched three-dimensional mathematical field drawn from five
recursive families: Mandelbrot, Julia, Apollonian gasket, Sierpiński triangle,
and Koch snowflake. Their 3D vocabulary uses recursively folded shells, tori,
boxes, cavities, and octahedra rather than presenting a flat textbook diagram.
A kaleidoscopic fold gives each vibration its own rotational order. The camera
travels through repeating architecture; the forward offset wraps at the same
spatial period as the geometry, producing a genuinely seamless loop.

The fractal is the observable structure of the space, not wallpaper. It leaves
a quiet sanctuary around the observer and fades before the outer frame and
copy. It should reward prolonged looking without competing with the foreground.

## Acceptance test for a scene

1. Turn it grayscale. Can the state still be distinguished?
2. Freeze one frame. Does the geometry imply its physics?
3. Hide the text. Does every moving element still have a role?
4. Hide the art. Is the sentence observational rather than motivational?
5. Compare it with the mobile app. Do line weight, darkness, warmth, and restraint belong to the same world?

## Reference-image translation

The supplied images are references for structural qualities, never palettes or
literal imitation:

- galaxy-like branching → high information flow across enormous negative space;
- nested perforated shells → recursion, permeability, and scale repetition;
- biological chambers → membranes that grow structure around voids;
- curling tendrils → directional gravity repeated at many scales;
- cosmic web → sparse nodes connected by field-line filaments;
- warped spacetime grid → spatial coherence visibly deformed by gravity.

The common DNA is recursion across scale, cavities, continuous surfaces, and
slow motion that can return exactly to its starting state for a seamless loop.

## Observer coupling

The canonical aura SVG is rendered as a transparent texture inside the Three.js
scene, not as interface above it. Its chest is the field origin: resonance,
permeability, and gravity determine how strongly nearby recursive space bends
around that origin. Field lines and particles share its world coordinates, so
the body can be occluded, surrounded, approached, or dissolved by the same
phenomenon it observes.
