# Instagram field protocol

## The content unit

One observation becomes a small three-part set:

1. **Reel (8–14 seconds):** phenomenon immediately visible; one uninterrupted observation; question enters late; tiny Selfinder mark in the final second.
2. **Portrait still:** the strongest frame, one question, no explanatory paragraph.
3. **Field note carousel:** still → 3–5 physical properties → a final open question. This is where the cosmology becomes collectible.

This creates repetition without reposting the same asset and lets the audience recognize each field before being asked to download anything.

## Reel timing

- 0.0s: the impossible phenomenon is already happening.
- 0–6s: no logo, title, or explanation; let attention settle.
- 6–10s: reveal one short observation question.
- final 0.8s: `SELFINDER`, quiet enough to reward attention.
- loop: final geometry and velocity should meet the opening frame.

## Editorial voice

Use questions about what is observable now. Avoid promises, rankings, diagnoses, instructions, “high/low vibration,” and claims that the system is scientific. Never tell viewers what state they are in.

## Engagement loop

Captions add one useful distinction, then ask for a description rather than a verdict: “Where do you notice contraction first?” is better than “Are you living in fear?” Pin thoughtful descriptions. Use replies as input for later field notes. Selfinder becomes a shared observatory, not an account broadcasting answers.

## First four-week sequence

- Week 1: Fear / Acceptance — contraction and permeability.
- Week 2: Grief / Love — lost and persistent connection.
- Week 3: Anger / Courage — force and directed motion.
- Week 4: Desire / Peace — pursuit and unhurried time.

Publish 2 reels and 1 still or carousel weekly. Paired contrasts teach the system faster than presenting all 17 in score order, and avoid implying a ladder from bad to good.
