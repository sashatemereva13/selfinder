## Summary of Key Decisions

**What worked:**

- Building GDPR rights into the foundation early — the erasure, export, and consent endpoints were already complete before data privacy became a submission requirement
- Groq as AI provider — free tier, low latency, OpenAI-compatible, good model quality
- Hardcoded narration + live AI conversation split — the most intentional architectural decision in the project, arrived at through iteration rather than upfront design
- Conventional commit prefixes — created a readable, chronological decision record without a formal project management tool

**What didn't work / dead ends:**

- Rooms architecture — three iterations over three months, ultimately cut after competitor research. The concept wasn't wrong; the direction was occupied
- Ambient AI endpoints — built, wired up, then converted to static text. Added latency and drift without proportional value
- GET /api/philosophers — built but superseded by a richer frontend dataset. Left in place, unused

**What I'd do differently:**

- Competitor research before architecture, not after. The rooms pivot cost three months of development that is now unlinked
- Rate limiting on auth routes from day one, not as a post-hoc gap
- Browser verification of full user paths after every flow change, not just code review of changed files
